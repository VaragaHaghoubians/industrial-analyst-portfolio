# Project brief

A planner looks at eight SKUs once a year and asks three questions.

- How many should we order at a time?
- When should we place that order?
- Which codes are already too high?

Finished goods are BRACKET, HOUSING and SUPPORT. Spares are BEARING, FILTER, SEAL-KIT, WELD-TIP and HYDRAULIC-HOSE. Demand is weekly for 2025, generated with seed 42.

The holding rate is the share of unit cost charged for keeping one unit for a year. Order cost is the cost of placing one order, not the price of the part.
