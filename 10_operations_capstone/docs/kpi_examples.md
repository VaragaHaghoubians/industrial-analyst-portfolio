# Examples you can do by hand

## Attainment and good units

Plan 100, actual 90, scrap 5.

- Attainment is 90 / 100 = 90%.
- Good units are 90 - 5 = 85.

If a second row has plan 50 and actual 50, total attainment is `(90 + 50) / (100 + 50) = 93.3%`. The average of 90% and 100% is 95%, and that is the wrong total.

## Order gap

Orders 108, good units 100. The gap is 100 - 108 = -8. Production is 8 units behind.

## A tiny t-test idea

You do not need to compute the p-value by hand. You do need to say what went into it: one number per day for the morning, one number per day for the night, both of them good units. The published means are 281.8 and 208.5.
